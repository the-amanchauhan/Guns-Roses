## Before you visit

Quick answers to common questions.

What should I bring?

Bring valid government ID. New Jersey law has permit and background check requirements for purchases, so call ahead to confirm what applies to you.

Do you buy or take trades?

Yes, customers have sold firearms to the shop and reported fair offers. Call or text for a quote.

Can I shop online?

Yes. Visit the web store at joehawk.com to browse inventory.

What are your hours?

Wednesday to Friday 1 PM to 7 PM, Saturday 12 PM to 5 PM. Closed Sunday to Tuesday.

All sales follow New Jersey and federal law. Please contact the shop for the requirements that apply to your purchase.
